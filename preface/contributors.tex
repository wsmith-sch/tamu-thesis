\section*{\bfseries Contributors}
A statement of contributors is required in this section.
It should include committee members and research collaborators, as well as any other individuals who made a meaningful contribution to the document.
Example language is provided below.

This work was supported by a thesis (or dissertation) committee consisting of Dr. Jane Doe [Chair -- also note if there is a Co-Chair] and Dr. John Doe of the Department of [Home Department] and Dr. XX and Dr. YY of the Department of [Other Department].

The data analyzed for Chapter 4 were provided by Dr. ZZ.
The analyses depicted in Chapter 5 were conducted in part by Dr. AA of the Department of Statistics and were published in (2004) in an article listed in the Journal of Things.

All other work conducted for the thesis or dissertation was completed by the student independently.

\section*{\bfseries Funding Sources}
A statement of funding source(s) is required in this section.
Provide any external or internal fellowships or grants that facilitated the research presented in your thesis or dissertation.
If no external funding was received, include a brief statement indicating that no outside funding was received.
