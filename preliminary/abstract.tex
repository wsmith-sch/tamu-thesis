The abstract should be no more than 350 words; submissions exceeding two pages will not be accepted.
This section provides a complete, concise snapshot of the research, addressing the purpose, methods, results, and conclusions, and must stand alone without formal citations or references to chapters or sections of the work.
Images, complex equations, and Greek letters or symbols should be avoided to ensure compatibility with a wide range of online databases that may index your thesis or dissertation.
Do not include citations, and do not label any subheadings as ``Abstract.”
If an alternative label is necessary, use ``Synopsis,” ``Overview,” or a similar term.

The abstract and the remainder of the manuscript are double-spaced, with no additional spacing before or after paragraphs.
Exceptions include the Title Page and secondary line items in the Table of Contents, List of Figures, and List of Tables, which are single-spaced.
The abstract begins on page ii, in lowercase Roman numerals.
